# AUDIT REPORT

Independent audit and release of Broker Growth OS. All data in the project is synthetic.

## Verdict

The builder's headline claims about test counts and structure were accurate, but two of the reported results were wrong because of generator and test defects: the planted Experiment A effect was weaker than claimed, and several tests could not detect real bugs. Everything below was reproduced, fixed at the root cause, and re-verified. Before the fixes, 155 tests were green and meant less than they appeared to: 6 of 39 deliberate bugs survived them.

## Bugs found: 16 (Sev-1: 2, Sev-2: 8, Sev-3: 6; 13 fixed, 3 Sev-3 left open)

Full table with root causes, commits and regression tests: [BUGS.md](BUGS.md).

- **B-03 Sev-1** `make all` crashed (or silently opened an empty database) when the output folder name contains `#`, `%` or `?`. SQLite URI built by string formatting.
- **B-05 Sev-1** The planted +30% effect for Experiment A was realised as about +24% (mean over 40 seeds: +23.5%) because only the treated probability was capped. The manifest, README and memo all claimed +30%. After the fix the 40-seed mean is +28.3%.
- **B-02 Sev-2** A lift of exactly 20% evaluated to 0.19999999999999996 and could not SHIP.
- **B-04 Sev-2** Deprecated Streamlit argument with a removal date that has passed.
- **B-06 Sev-2** Multiple comparisons, "one draw" and generous-funnel caveats were missing from deliverables; broken screenshot link.
- **B-07 Sev-2** Six mutants survived (dormancy and 14-day boundaries, `p <= alpha`, "sample reached" on the larger arm, AI scorer weights); four more were only caught indirectly.
- **B-09 Sev-2** `dedupe_agencies` merged same-name agencies if given raw blank states.
- **B-10 Sev-2** Two tests asserted "referral is the best source" on one noisy draw of 113 leads. Replaced by a large-n test of the planted ordering (a test was changed; reasoning in BUGS.md).
- **B-12 Sev-2** Unsupported claim about broker behaviour in the memo.
- **B-16 Sev-2** Found by random test ordering: `make metrics` after `make clean` silently reported 0 dormant agencies; and a test mutated shared state so the suite only passed in file order.
- Sev-3: B-01 file-handle leak in tests, B-08 untested bootstrap/filters (tests added), B-11 `write=False` created directories, B-13 11 vs 12 commits, B-14 first-load bootstrap race, B-15 personal email in commit authorship.

## Mutations that initially survived

M03b (weekly-SQL dormancy `>=` to `>`), M19 (`<` to `<=`), M07 (14-day activation `<=` to `<`), M14 (`p < alpha` to `<=`), M15 (sample size "reached" using the larger arm), M31 (AI scorer weights swapped). All are now killed. Also killed only indirectly before: M16 naive relative CI, M17 Tier-1 threshold, M22 source map, M33 no blocked randomization.

## Experiment decision rates (40 simulated seeds, [docs/seed_sweep.json](docs/seed_sweep.json))

| Experiment | SHIP | ITERATE | INCONCLUSIVE | p < 0.05 | mean relative lift (planted) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A ICP-targeted outbound | 32 | 8 | 0 | 36 | +28.3% (+30%) |
| B submission-ready onboarding | 0 | 14 | 26 | 14 | +13.9% (+15%) |
| C reactivation (null) | 0 | 3 | 37 | 3 | +13.5% (0%; ratio estimator on a tiny base) |

Before the B-05 fix: A SHIP 29/40, mean +23.5%. The builder's "5 of 8 seeds" referred to seeds 42 and 1-7 on the pre-fix generator; seeds 1-8 gave 6/8. Seed 42 is one draw (A +37.5%, B +11.8% INCONCLUSIVE, C 0.0% INCONCLUSIVE). Stratified randomization was introduced after seed 42 first showed B at -11%; the strata are baseline covariates only, the reason is logged (D25), and it reduces variance without changing the planted effects (B's 40-seed mean is +13.9% vs +15% planted).

## Numbers that changed and why

All headline numbers changed because B-05 changed the generated data: first submissions 521 to 486, bound 203 to 209, Experiment A +27.3% to +37.5%, Experiment B +20.5% (ITERATE) to +11.8% (INCONCLUSIVE), tiers 82/610/602/206 to 83/610/614/193, and the best source on seed 42 is now partner (21.5%) rather than referral (18.6%; referral is best at large n, tested). The memo, Loom script and data dictionary were regenerated by script; FINAL_REPORT was edited by hand and is now guarded by a drift test. The previously reported figures were first verified as correct for the old data.

## Multiple comparisons

Three experiments at alpha 0.05 with no correction: now stated in README, memo, Loom script and dashboard. Over 40 seeds the null experiment C looks significant in 3 (7.5%).
